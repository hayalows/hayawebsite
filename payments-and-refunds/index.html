Payments and refunds

How payments, cancellations, product concerns and refund requests are currently handled by Hayalows Ventures.

These terms explain the current payment process for Hayalows Ventures. Contact us before paying when anything is unclear.

On this page

1. Making a payment

Customers should use the payment route that matches what they are paying for. The Hayalows Ventures payment option is for agreed services. The 50's Bliss Storefront is for browsing and placing a new product order, while the 50's Bliss order payment option is for an order already confirmed through WhatsApp, phone, delivery or in person.

Paystack processes online payments independently. Hayalows does not ask for or receive a customer's Mobile Money PIN, bank card PIN or full card details.

2. Paying the correct amount

Before paying, confirm the business, purpose and amount. For a Hayalows service or an existing 50's Bliss order, the amount should already have been agreed. A customer who is unsure should contact Hayalows before completing payment.

Customers should use their correct name and keep the Paystack confirmation and transaction reference.

3. Service deposits and balances

Payments for Hayalows services may represent a deposit, part payment, final balance or another amount agreed for the work. The purpose of the payment should be clear in the service conversation before payment is made.

A deposit may allow planning or work to begin. Work already planned, reserved, purchased or completed may affect what can be refunded if a project changes or is cancelled.

4. 50's Bliss product payments

Customers placing a new order should use the Storefront when the required products are available there. Customers paying for an order agreed through another channel should confirm the products, quantity, total amount and any delivery charge before using the existing-order payment option.

Product availability can change. A payment does not replace the need to confirm any details that were unclear at the time of ordering.

5. Incorrect or duplicate payments

Contact Hayalows promptly if you paid the wrong business, entered the wrong amount or believe a payment was duplicated. Provide your full name, payment date, amount, purpose and Paystack transaction reference.

Hayalows will review the payment and the surrounding circumstances. A correction or refund is not automatic and may depend on whether work, an order, delivery or another cost has already begun.

6. Service cancellations

If you need to cancel or pause a Hayalows service, contact us as soon as possible. The work already completed, time reserved, third-party costs and materials already prepared may be considered when the remaining balance or any possible refund is reviewed.

Where a separate written proposal or agreement applies, its project-specific terms may also be relevant.

7. Refund requests

Refund requests are reviewed individually. Explain what happened and include the relevant payment reference and supporting information. Hayalows may need to confirm the payment with Paystack and review any work, product, delivery or cost already involved.

No refund is promised automatically. If a refund is approved, the method and timing will be communicated after the review.

8. Physical product concerns

For a 50's Bliss product concern, contact Hayalows with the order details and a clear explanation. The review may consider whether the product was opened, used, damaged after delivery, delivered incorrectly or different from what was confirmed.

Photos or other reasonable evidence may be requested to understand the concern. The available resolution will depend on the circumstances.

9. Delivery charges

Delivery charges should be confirmed before payment where possible. They may not be refundable after dispatch, travel or delivery work has begun, even when a product order later changes.

10. Failed or pending payments

Do not pay again immediately if Paystack shows a pending result or if you are unsure whether a payment completed. Check for a Paystack confirmation, review your payment account and contact Hayalows with the transaction reference.

Hayalows will not treat a payment as successful only because a customer returned to this website. Confirmation must come from the payment record.

11. How to contact Hayalows

For payment support, email info@hayalows.com or use the support options on the payment page.
