Privacy

How Hayalows handles information

A plain-English explanation of the information Hayalows may receive when you contact us, request work, place an order or make a payment.

Hayalows only asks for information that helps us respond, provide a service, manage an order or resolve a payment concern. Do not send PINs or full card details.

1. Information we may receive

When you use the website, WhatsApp or email to contact Hayalows, you may provide your name, business name, phone number, email address and an explanation of what you need.

For services and 50's Bliss orders, Hayalows may also receive information about the requested work, products, quantities, delivery arrangements, agreed prices and relevant conversations.

For payment support, we may ask for the amount, date, purpose and Paystack transaction reference. This helps us identify the correct record without asking for sensitive payment credentials.

2. Why Hayalows keeps these details

Information may be used to:

  • respond to questions and enquiries;
  • prepare, deliver and support agreed services;
  • confirm, fulfil or review product orders;
  • keep practical business, transaction and communication records;
  • resolve payment, delivery or service concerns; and
  • improve how Hayalows communicates and works.

Hayalows does not currently operate customer accounts through this website.

3. Payments and Paystack

Online payment links on this website lead to Paystack, an independent payment processor. Paystack handles the payment interface and may process information under its own terms and privacy practices.

Hayalows may receive a transaction record, payment status, customer name, amount and reference needed to reconcile or support a payment. Hayalows does not receive or store a customer's full card details, bank card PIN or Mobile Money PIN through this website.

4. How long information is kept

Hayalows may keep service, order, communication and payment records for as long as they remain reasonably useful for providing the work, maintaining business records, resolving concerns or meeting applicable responsibilities.

There is no single retention period stated here because different records may be needed for different lengths of time. Information that is no longer reasonably needed may be deleted or reduced where practical.

5. When information may be shared

Information may be shared with a service provider when it is reasonably needed to provide a requested service, process a payment, arrange delivery or maintain the website. Hayalows should only share what is relevant to that purpose.

Information may also be disclosed where reasonably required to protect people, address fraud or comply with a valid legal requirement. Hayalows does not claim to sell customer contact details.

6. Security limitations

Hayalows takes reasonable care with the information it handles, but no website, email, messaging platform or storage method can be promised to be completely secure. Customers should avoid sending passwords, PINs, full card details or other unnecessary sensitive information.

If you believe information has been sent to Hayalows in error or may be at risk, contact us promptly.

7. Correction and deletion requests

You may ask Hayalows to correct inaccurate contact, service or order information. You may also request deletion of information, although some records may need to be kept where they remain reasonably necessary for an active service, transaction, dispute, record-keeping responsibility or legal requirement.

Hayalows may need to confirm that the request comes from the person connected to the information before acting.

8. Contact Hayalows

For a privacy question or request, email info@hayalows.com and explain what you would like us to review.
