Hayalows Ventures is led by Papa Kojo Mensah. The work brings together design, communication and practical technology to help a business explain its offer and make its next steps easier.

The name comes from the idea of a higher low. Progress can rise and fall, but each useful improvement should leave a stronger foundation behind.

We start with the problem in front of us, agree on a manageable scope and build from there. Where a project needs other skills, we discuss that as part of the plan.
