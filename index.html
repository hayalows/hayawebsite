A few good questions / 05

Before you ask.

Still unsure? Let's talk it through ↗

Do I need a new logo or website?+

Maybe. We first look at what is getting in the way. Sometimes clearer service descriptions or a better follow-up process will do more than a complete rebuild.

How much will my project cost?+

Each project is quoted in Ghana cedis after we agree on the scope. You will know what is included, what is separate, the payment stages and the timing before work begins.

Can we start with something small?+

Yes. A single page, a clearer offer or a simple tracker can be a useful first project. We can agree on a smaller scope and build from there.

Are hosting and domain costs included?+

Hosting, domain registration and any paid tools are listed separately in your quote where they apply. We agree on who owns the accounts and what renewal costs to expect.

How do we begin?+

Tell us what your business does, what is happening now and what you want to improve. WhatsApp or the guided enquiry is enough. You do not need a finished brief.
