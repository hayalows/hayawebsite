Working with Hayalows

Practical terms for using this website, working with Hayalows Ventures and buying 50's Bliss products.

Project details agreed in a written proposal, message or other direct conversation may add to these general terms.

1. Business and website use

Hayalows is the public-facing brand of Hayalows Ventures. These terms apply to this website, Hayalows services and 50's Bliss product activity managed through Hayalows Ventures.

You may use the website to understand the business, contact Hayalows, review public policies and reach verified external payment pages. Do not misuse the website, interfere with its operation or present its content as your own.

2. Hayalows services

Hayalows may provide branding, design, websites, digital tools, marketing support, business advisory and related practical work. The exact service depends on what is agreed for a project.

Information on this website is general. It is not a promise that every service, result, timeline or approach will suit every customer.

3. Customer responsibilities

Customers should provide accurate information, make decisions and approvals within a reasonable time, supply materials they have permission to use and explain important constraints before work begins.

Customers are responsible for reviewing names, prices, dates, contact details and other factual information before approving work for publication or use.

4. Agreeing on scope and price

The work, deliverables, price and payment stages should be agreed through a proposal, email, WhatsApp conversation or another clear record. If a detail is important but has not been agreed, contact Hayalows before paying or expecting work to begin.

Any third-party costs, subscriptions, printing, hosting, advertising, delivery or purchases should be identified separately where they apply.

5. Deposits and balances

Hayalows service payments may be deposits, part payments, final balances or another agreed stage. A deposit may be required before planning, purchasing or production begins.

Customers should pay the correct amount through the correct payment route and keep the payment reference. Outstanding balances may need to be settled before final files, delivery, publication or continued work.

6. Project changes, timelines and delays

A request outside the agreed scope may require a revised price, timeline or separate phase. Hayalows should explain a material change before carrying it out.

Timelines may depend on customer feedback, access, content, approvals, third-party services and other circumstances. If either party causes a delay, the schedule may need to change. Hayalows does not promise a fixed completion date unless it has been clearly agreed.

7. Revisions

Reasonable revisions should relate to the agreed purpose and scope. A substantial change of direction, repeated work after approval or new deliverable may be treated as additional work.

Where a proposal states a revision process, that project-specific process applies.

8. Intellectual property

Customers should only supply text, images, logos, music, data or other material they own or are allowed to use. The customer remains responsible for permissions connected to supplied material.

Ownership and permitted use of final Hayalows work should follow the project agreement and payment status. Hayalows may retain its underlying methods, reusable systems, working files and general know-how unless a different arrangement is clearly agreed.

9. 50's Bliss products and delivery

50's Bliss product availability, sizes, quantities and prices can change. Customers should review the current Storefront information or confirm an order directly before paying.

Delivery location, charge and timing should be agreed where delivery is required. Delivery timing may be affected by distance, availability and the delivery provider. Product concerns should be reported with the order details and reasonable supporting information.

10. Payment processing through Paystack

Paystack independently processes online payments reached from this website. Customers may also use the displayed USSD codes on supported networks. Hayalows does not collect card details or PINs on this website and will not ask customers to send them.

A return to this website does not prove that a payment succeeded. Payment status must be confirmed from the transaction record.

11. Cancellations and refunds

Cancellations and refund requests are reviewed according to the circumstances, including work completed, time reserved, products used or opened, delivery activity and costs already committed.

Refunds are not automatic. The more detailed current process is explained on the payments and refunds page.

12. Limitation of responsibility

Hayalows aims to provide useful and careful work, but business results also depend on customer decisions, market conditions, third-party platforms and how work is used. Hayalows does not promise a particular level of sales, profit, ranking, audience response or business success.

Where allowed by applicable law, responsibility should be considered in relation to the specific service or product involved and the circumstances of the issue. Nothing here removes a responsibility that cannot legally be excluded.

13. Ghana and contact

These general terms are intended to operate under the laws and jurisdiction of Ghana. If a concern arises, both parties should first try to understand and resolve it through clear communication.

Questions about these terms can be sent to info@hayalows.com.
