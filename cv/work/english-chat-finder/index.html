Where it is now
Live. Version 1.8.

The problem

BYU-Pathway students book 30-minute English Chat sessions with volunteers. Each volunteer has their own booking page.

I had to open booking pages one after another to find an open time. I wanted one search that could check them together and show the nearest options first.

What it does

  • Reads the official list of volunteers.
  • Checks each booking page for the next 60 days.
  • Shows open times for this week, next week and later.
  • Sends you to Google to book. The official page always has the final word.

Watch a search.

Pretend volunteers, pretend times. The idea is real: check a few at a time, then show the soonest first.

The real app checks ten calendars at once, and slows down if Google asks it to.

Limits I built in

I chose to check calendars in small groups instead of sending every request at once. If Google slows the requests, the finder waits before trying again.

The app keeps results on your own device for a short time. It does not need an account or track who books a session.

A small honest note

This is an independent helper I made as a student. It is not an official BYU-Pathway tool.
