Why I made it

In Ghana, when someone says “I am not okay”, they often get laughed at, doubted or lectured. Many never try again.

Most campaigns say “talk to someone”. Nobody teaches the someone. StigmaClear teaches the someone.

What is inside

  • Gyina Me Ho. Five moves for the moment someone opens up.
  • CLEAR words. Better ways to talk about mental health.
  • Mind Weather. A gentle check-in for a friend, a child or yourself.
  • Nkra. Short, kind letters from people who have been there.

Design choices

Big, plain words. Warm colours. One clear next step.

A person in a hard moment should never hunt for help. The free helpline, 0800 678 678, sits near the top of the home page and in the footer of every page.

Built with care

Every fact has a source. Every letter is read by a person before it goes up. I built it to load fast on cheap phones and slow data, because that is how most people will find it.
