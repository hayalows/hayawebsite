02 · Live product

RouteLab

A map-first tool for planning routes and remembering everyday movement in one calmer flow.

Visit product ↗

Choose a part of the story.

Problem01

The problem

Route and place tools can become dense quickly. RouteLab started from wanting a calmer way to think about routes and places without turning a simple trip into a noisy dashboard.

Approach02

The approach

Keep the map as the main working surface and bring planning and movement history into the same flow. The interface direction is deliberately quieter so the route remains the thing the user is thinking about.

Evidence03

What the work demonstrates

Product framing, information hierarchy and restraint. It is also an example of choosing what not to surface at once.

04 · Live tool

English Chat Finder

A single search across availability from more than 200 English-conversation calendars.

Open tool ↗

Choose a part of the story.

Problem01

The problem

Finding a suitable conversation session meant checking many calendars repeatedly. The work was simple in theory but repetitive in practice.

Approach02

The approach

Move the user’s actual question, “what session can I join at this time?”, to the front of the experience. Availability from 200+ calendars is brought into one search instead of making the user visit them one by one.

Evidence03

What the work demonstrates

Turning a repeated manual task into a focused utility, with search as the main interaction instead of the underlying calendar structure.
