What caught my eye

David presents himself as a design engineer taking work from idea to production. The page mixes service areas, previous work and client comments.

What works well

The value of the positioning is clear: it tells potential clients what they could ask him to build. Client testimonials are presented as quotes on the site, not independently verified endorsements.

My recommendation

Look for a complete SaaS or business-website project. Pay attention to how design choices and technical delivery are connected.
