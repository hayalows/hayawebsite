ELEna Labs

What caught my eye

Ena connects product management, software, music and live events on one page. The DJ-style navigation gives the site character without hiding the work and experience sections.

What works well

The portfolio explains the kind of problems Ena works on and includes project stories such as Kickback. It helps a visitor see a person with practical interests beyond a job title. Reported launch figures need independent confirmation.

My recommendation

Open the work section and look at Kickback. Focus on what Ena says she owned, what was shipped and how the outcome was measured.
