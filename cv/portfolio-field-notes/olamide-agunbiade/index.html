What caught my eye

Olamide leads with illustration and art rather than a product-engineering résumé. The categories include characters, portraits, commissions and watercolour.

What works well

The image-led approach fits work where visual judgment matters most. Visitors can browse by type of artwork without having to work through a long biography.

My recommendation

Start with the illustration archive. Compare how line, colour and character change across different pieces rather than judging the portfolio from one image.
