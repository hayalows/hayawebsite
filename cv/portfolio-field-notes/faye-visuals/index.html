What caught my eye

Faye moves between brand, product design and code. The page tells you that direction plainly and separates product work from brand and motion work.

What works well

The work categories give a broad practice some order. I appreciate seeing live builds alongside design presentations because visitors can compare the intention with the product that was actually made.

My recommendation

Open the selected product projects before the motion experiments. Look for the problem, the role and the decisions that carried through into the build.
