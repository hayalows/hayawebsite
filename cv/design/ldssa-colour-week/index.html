Multi-year event graphics

LDSSA Colour Week

Present the Colour Week activities in a welcoming way while making the daily programme clear.

The design decisions

Colourful lettering and lively shapes set the mood. The activity designs put the day-by-day information first, so people can see what is happening. These pieces belong to different versions of the event, and I'll add the exact year to each one when I've checked the artwork.

Designer's note

An event design needs to invite people in and help them understand where they need to be.

Looking at the finished work

The artwork shows a recurring event identity and practical information across two editions.
