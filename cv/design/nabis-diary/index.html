The brief

Give a personal publication an identity and a way to celebrate its readers.

The design decisions

The ND letterform and soft violet setting establish the publication's mood. The brighter 70+ subscribers graphic gives a different moment a more celebratory feel.

Designer’s note

The identity could feel quiet while the milestone could celebrate the people reading.

Looking at the finished work

The 70+ subscribers figure appears on the original milestone artwork; it is not presented as growth caused by the design.
