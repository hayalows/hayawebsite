About

Clear first. Then memorable.

Since 2022 I have run Iconka Designs from Kumasi, making brand identities, event campaigns and social graphics for small businesses, student associations and community events.

I design for the person reading the work: what they notice first, what they need to understand, and what they should do next. Most of my projects start with a message that has to land quickly, on a phone screen or a printed poster.

  • Brand identities
  • Event campaigns and countdowns
  • Posters and information graphics
  • Social media graphics
