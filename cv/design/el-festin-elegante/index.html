Event campaign and countdown

El Festín Elegante

Introduce the dinner and awards event, then keep the information familiar as the date gets closer.

The design decisions

The main poster uses a dark palette, gold lettering and a clear information area. I carried that treatment into the five countdown designs, changing the day number while keeping the campaign recognisable.

Designer's note

The countdown gave me a way to repeat the message without redesigning the whole poster each day.
